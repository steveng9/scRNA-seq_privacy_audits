6. *** FUTURE WORK / POST-CAMDA-2026 DIRECTIONS (added 2026-07-27) ***
   Do NOT put these in the rebuttal (see 5(b)); track here until completed. Ordered by priority.

   (1) RENAME scMAMA-MIA -> "MahalaMIA" and generalize it to a MIA against ANY multivariate SDG
       method, not just single-cell. Rationale: the core statistic is the Mahalanobis distance to a
       fitted covariance, which is not single-cell-specific. In particular, adapt/apply it to BULK
       RNA-seq generators: bulk is a single multivariate distribution per sample (no per-cell-type
       copula stratification needed), so the Mahalanobis attack applies DIRECTLY. Validation: the
       Mahalanobis attack was applied directly to bulk RNA-seq and WON CAMDA 2026 Track 1 (bulk) --
       so this generalization is already empirically demonstrated, not speculative. Work items:
       rename package/modules/paper terminology (scmamamia -> mahalamia), factor out the
       single-cell-specific pieces (cell-type stratification, per-cell-type copula) so the attack
       runs on a plain multivariate SDG, and write up the bulk RNA-seq case as a second domain.

   (2) ADD NEW SDG METHODS to the attack sweep (extends CLAUDE.md Experiment #2). Candidates:
       C2S (Cell2Sentence), scGFT, scLDM, and a vine-copula NMF variant. Goal: broaden the
       cross-model transferability evidence (JvKY W4) beyond the current six generators and probe
       which architectural features make a generator vulnerable vs. resistant to MahalaMIA.

   (3) PCA-BEFORE-MAHALANOBIS. Apply PCA (dimensionality reduction / decorrelation) to the
       expression / covariance structure before computing the Mahalanobis distance, rather than
       running Mahalanobis directly in gene space. Motivation: better-conditioned covariance
       (fewer degenerate / singular directions than genes>cells), potential accuracy gain, and a
       cleaner story than the current pseudo-inverse + nearest-PSD handling.
